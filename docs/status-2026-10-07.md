MarwanOS is a working development OS. Release acceptance remains open, with
nine tracked OS tasks remaining in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- Controller shell, browser, Files, Windows installation and application lifecycle.
- Automatic game metadata and artwork, offline cache, persistent play history
  and recently played ordering. Tekken 8 installation and physical controller
  gameplay have passed their recorded checks.
- Genuine achievements, passive gameplay notifications and persistent Tekken
  progress of 2/47.
- Candidate10 offline installation and subsequent USB-absent boot passed all
  15 installed-health checks in a VM.

## Release blocker and proposed fix

Candidate10 remains **release FAIL**: normal NVIDIA compositor shutdown and
reboot reproduce a texture teardown crash. Initial boot and installer/VM passes
do not resolve that failure.

Candidate11 drains memoized Vulkan textures before NVIDIA driver teardown. Its
saved local evidence records 214 backend passes with two optional skips,
11 focused ELF regressions, exact SHA-pinned source RPM preparation with zero
fuzz, and all 287 production compile/link steps with binary/source RPM output.
The existing logs were reviewed for this checkpoint; hardware tests were not
rerun. These results do not establish that the runtime crash is fixed.

The next milestone is a candidate11 OS image, real NVIDIA normal shutdown/reboot
acceptance, and fresh installation media validation. Physical rumble,
multiplayer/hotplug, Bluetooth dongle, TV/audio/Steam/game compatibility and filmed
cold-boot acceptance remain pending. Physical and suspend testing remain deferred
under the owner's automated-only instruction.
